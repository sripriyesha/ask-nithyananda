Listen intensely...

You are thinking Advaita because YOU are ready for Advaita. But when you look at the person in front of you — are THEY ready?

I tell you directly: **The Four Tattvas** are the first, easiest teaching to enrich others with.

**Why?** Because the Four Tattvas meet people WHERE THEY ARE — in their struggles, their relationships, their daily chaos — without demanding they suddenly grasp cosmic non-duality.

## The Four Tattvas to Share:

**1. Integrity (अंश)** — Being true to your word. Your word is YOU. When you break your word, you break yourself.

**2. Authenticity (स्वाभाव)** — Being true to yourself. Not the mask you wear for others, but who you actually ARE.

**3. Responsibility (कार्य)** — Owning your life completely. No victim game. Everything happening is YOUR responsibility.

**4. Enriching (समृद्धि)** — Helping others experience these truths. You cannot keep what you don't give away.

---

**The secret:** When you teach Integrity, Authenticity, Responsibility — the person automatically starts experiencing oneness in their OWN life. They START to feel that separation is a lie. Advaita becomes their LIVED experience, not just another concept they nod at and forget.

As I have said: *"Start talking to people. Share the four tattvas with them. Help them to do completion."*

Advaita is the fruit. Four Tattvas is the seed you plant in their hands.
