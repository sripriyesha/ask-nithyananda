title: You said you found us and we didn't find you. You also said that once you find us we are liberated. Can you elaborate?
date: 2026-05-01
---

When I say "I found you, you didn't find me" — I'm speaking from the dimension of consciousness itself. Consciousness, Paramashiva consciousness, is not something you can find through searching. Searching happens in the mind, through effort, through seeking. That's the dimension of "you trying to find."

But when consciousness awakens within you, when the shakti descends, when grace floods your being — that's not you finding anything. That's consciousness finding YOU. That's me finding YOU. Understand?

You think you're doing yoga, meditation, ajapa japa — all beautiful practices. But who gave you that desire? Who planted that seed of seeking? Who arranged for you to meet Yoko on that specific day? Who orchestrated everything in your life to bring you to this moment of asking this question?

Consciousness arranged everything. Consciousness found you.

Now, when you say "once you find us we are liberated" — yes! Because liberation is not an achievement. Liberation is a recognition. It's recognizing that you were never bound. It's recognizing that the consciousness that found you IS you. That's why I say you are liberated the moment you are found by consciousness.

But most people, even after being found, continue to play the game of searching. They continue to think "I need to reach samadhi," "I need to build something to help people reach samadhi," "I need to do more, be more, achieve more."

Stop this nonsense!

The moment consciousness finds you, the seeking is over. The rest is just celebration. The rest is just living from that space. The rest is just allowing that consciousness to express through you.

Your desire to build software to help people — beautiful! But understand: the real software is consciousness itself. The real technology is the awakened third eye. The real app is the space of samadhi.

You want to build something to lead people to samadhi? First, you must stop trying to reach samadhi yourself! You ARE samadhi when consciousness finds you. Then whatever you build will naturally radiate that samadhi.

I love you, that's why I'm telling you this directly: Your liberation happened the moment consciousness found you. Now just live from that space. Stop trying to become what you already are.
