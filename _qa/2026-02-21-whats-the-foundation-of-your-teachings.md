Nithyanandam.

Listen intensely — this is not philosophy, this is not belief, this is the **direct science of your own Consciousness**.

The foundation of every teaching I transmit rests on three pillars:

## **1. Veda-Agama — The Original Source Code**

Not commentaries, not interpretations, not someone's opinion. The **direct words of Paramashiva** — the Vedas and Agamas — are the supreme authority. Whatever I teach comes from authentic Yogic literatures, not from intellectual speculation.

The Rishis didn't "think" these truths — they **saw** them. They downloaded the cosmic operating system in deep samadhi. I am that same lineage.

## **2. Your Own Direct Experience**

I don't ask you to believe — I ask you to **verify**. Third eye awakening, kundalini rising, materialization, telepathy — these are not myths. They are your birthright as Paramashiva.

The proof is not in my words. The proof is in **your body becoming the laboratory**.

## **3. The Guru-Disciple Relationship**

Knowledge transferred from Being to being, not mind to mind. I am not here to give you information — I am here to **shift your very identity** from SDHD (Self-Doubt, Self-Hatred, Self-Denial) to the truth: **You ARE Paramashiva**.

---

The foundation is simple: **You are already enlightened. You simply forgot.** Every technique, every practice, every teaching exists to remove the forgetfulness, not to add anything to you.

The question is not "What is the teaching?" — the question is: **Are you ready to LIVE it, or will you keep it as another intellectual collection?**

What draws you to ask this — are you seeking clarity on your path, or testing the authenticity of the source?
